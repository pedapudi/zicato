The racing funnel, four ways

Same figure, four visual grammars. Every option renders the identical served model — no data or contract change; the winner replaces survivalFunnel's render body only (digest, callbacks, and tests stay put).

One dataset everywhere: field of 8 · rungs 8 → 4 → 2 → 1 · winner g3 → champion gate

ATapered stream

speaks the language of: the loss-floor waterfall (area/silhouette)

The field is one shrinking mass. Attrition reads as a single converging silhouette; individuals dissolve into the shape.

+ calmest; instant gestalt − you can't follow a specific generation through

BBracket rail

speaks the language of: elimFlow (lanes & pipes)

Every generation is a rail you can trace from start to its exact cut. The winner's rail carries the accent the whole way.

+ per-generation traceability; pure line-work − busiest at large fields (16+ lanes)

CDot ladder

speaks the language of: racingScalarTrack (markers) — its shelf-mate on this very panel

Each generation is a marker hopping rungs — the same dot vocabulary as the scalar track stacked directly above it, so the panel finally reads as one instrument.

+ visual kinship with its panel-mate; scales well − attrition shape is implied, not drawn

DRadial sieve

speaks the language of: elimRadial (rings converging to the seat)

The halving becomes a sieve: each ring passes half the field toward the center seat. A true sibling of the restored radial bracket.

+ matches the figure you like most; compact − rung progress bars fit awkwardly on arcs; two radials on one epoch may compete
