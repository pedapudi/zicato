The judge audit is the validity workhorse. For one judge under test, the independent meta-judge's adjudications over every decision fill a 2×2 confusion matrix (TP / FP / FN / TN), yielding precision / recall / F1 / FPR. A κ gauge reports the judge's self-consistency over replicated runs of the same transcript — a judge below threshold is unreliable regardless of its precision. Evidence chips link each mis-fire straight to the offending span. A small cross-judge graph exposes redundancy (corr≈1 ⇒ prune) and systematic conflict. Generic content throughout.

Part of the reflection / "Instrument lens" design study — mockups for board-reflection (Measurement-System-Analysis for the evaluation contract). Companion to the board-reflection design note, not a TODO. ← study index.
