theme

The transcript x-ray is the reflection layer's centerpiece. A judge fires (or stays silent) over a real run; the independent meta-judge reads the same transcript and adjudicates — confirm (the failure is there) or deny (nothing happened where the judge marked). Every verdict is span-grounded so the operator verifies in seconds. The colour grammar is the whole point: TP = a quiet-green seam under a confirmed span; FP = a red mark where the judge fired but nothing happened (false fire); FN = a highlighted missed span the judge stayed silent on.
