The corpus grid is the live face of an active reflect run. Reflection executes the board with replication and adjudication — a long, budget-spending process — so the operator watches the observation corpus fill in: a (candidate × entry × replicate) grid where each cell advances queued → running → observed → adjudicated, the adjudicated cells already carrying their TP/FP/FN tint. It is the progress surface that respects the render-discipline rule (no DOM rebuild on a no-op heartbeat — only changed cells repaint). Two variants: the fill-in grid mid-run, and a per-stage progress rail.

Part of the reflection / "Instrument lens" design study — mockups for board-reflection (Measurement-System-Analysis for the evaluation contract). Companion to the board-reflection design note, not a TODO. ← study index.
