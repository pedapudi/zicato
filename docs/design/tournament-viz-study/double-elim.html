zicato — double-elimination visualization study

theme

Eight options for visualizing a double-elimination tournament epoch, each rendered three ways: a compact hero-banner (top-bar-width, at-a-glance standings), a cross-round / epoch-level view (the whole epoch across its rounds), and a detailed round-level bracket. All eight render the SAME mid-tournament sample — one champion v3 protected, eight challengers v4…v11, some undefeated in the winners' bracket, some dropped to the losers' bracket (one loss), two eliminated (two losses), and a grand final pending. Every option is built to make first-class the two facts the current dashboard buries: which bracket side a gen is on (winners' vs losers') and lives remaining (first life vs second life). Switch the theme — every figure recolours from the active tokens.
