↩ structures · zoom: cross-round theme

The cross-round zoom sits above any single tournament and below the whole epoch: it tracks the sequence of evolve-rounds within one epoch, each of which mints a field of generations and resolves to a single promote / hold. It is deliberately structure-agnostic — the same view reads correctly whether each round was a gauntlet, a Swiss, a bracket, or a race, because all it consumes is the per-round summary every structure already emits: {champion, challengers[], gateOutcome, structure} plus the derived loss-floor waterfall and champion reign spans. The operator's four questions: (1) is the loss floor descending round over round? (2) which rounds PROMOTED vs HELD? (3) who is the reigning champion and for how long — where are the handoffs? (4) was a round's champion re-run (live) or its score reused (cached)? Below: 6 first-principles options. One realistic sample, identical across all 6. Lower scalar = better; descending floor / promote = good.
