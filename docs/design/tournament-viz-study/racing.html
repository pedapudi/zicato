theme

Six visual treatments of one live RACING tournament — each shown in three views: a compact hero banner (at-a-glance standings), a cross-rung funnel (the field narrowing across rungs), and a round-level rung detail. The race duels every challenger against the champion benchmark on a board subset; after each rung it cuts the worst by scalar rank (keep top 1/eta) — elimination is by rank, not a gate. Survivors re-race on a larger board slice; the final survivor faces a full-board racing-final champion gate. Every option encodes the two things the current funnel drops: scalar magnitude (how close a cut was) and board_fraction (the evidence weight behind each rung). wins/losses are always 0 for racing and are not shown.

This is the structure-specific tournament FIGURE at three zooms — the SINGLE-ROUND layer. The hero shell, cross-epoch, cross-round (across evolve-rounds), single-matchup, and single-generation levels are structure-agnostic — see their own pages (← index).
