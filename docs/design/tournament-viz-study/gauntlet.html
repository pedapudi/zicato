The gauntlet is the degenerate base case of every richer tournament figure: a funnel / bracket of exactly one match — 1 champion vs 1 challenger, one full-board duel, promote-on-gate. There are no in-flight standings to project; the standings are trivially the incumbent vs the one challenger. The operator's whole question collapses to four signals: did the challenger clear the gate, by how much (delta_scalar magnitude + sign — negative = challenger better, must beat promote_margin), on how many boards (pass/fail spread), and how reliably (replicate variance). Below: 6 genuinely different visual takes, each in 3 views — (a) hero-banner, (b) single-round (the one duel, board by board), (c) within-round duel detail.
