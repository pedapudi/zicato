Six distinct ways to render a SWISS tournament — the configurable per-epoch structure where the reigning champion participates as a contestant, no one is eliminated, everyone plays every round, and an odd field grants a BYE = +1 free Copeland point. Standing is the Copeland score (duels won) tie-broken by mean scalar (lower = better), then id. After rounds_n rounds the top non-champion leader faces the swiss-final champion-gate and is crowned only if it clears it. Every option below renders one identical mid-tournament dataset: 7 contestants (champion + 6 challengers), 4 rounds, round 4 still in flight. Each option carries three views — (a) hero banner (at-a-glance live leaderboard), (b) cross-round (standings trajectory across the Swiss rounds), and (c) round-level (per-round pairings + standings).

What we are designing against: the current frontend invents phantom draws and shows an always-0 losses column (both dropped here — the backend never draws and losses are structurally always 0); its bump chart is the strongest standings idiom but is epoch-only; the live ladder leads with pairings and buries standings; and the mean-scalar tiebreak — the real ranking signal — is hidden under Copeland points. So here the Copeland leaderboard + rank trajectory are the hero, the mean-scalar tiebreak is surfaced as a magnitude, and the champion is marked in the field.

This is the structure-specific tournament FIGURE at three zooms — the SINGLE-ROUND layer. The hero shell, cross-epoch, cross-round (across evolve-rounds), single-matchup, and single-generation levels are structure-agnostic — see their own pages (← index).
